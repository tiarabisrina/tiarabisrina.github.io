CASE STUDY 02  /  UX RESEARCH  /  E-COMMERCE & RESELLER

Making the EIGER reseller journey easier to trust

UX research on how customers, resellers and the EIGER team buy, sell and manage orders through the EIGER reseller platform.

01 / Context

One platform, three user perspectives

EIGER sells through several channels, including resellers who share products with their own followers and contacts. This project studied how the reseller platform works for three groups: customers who buy through a reseller link, resellers who share products and track orders, and the EIGER team who support them. The goal was to find what makes the experience easier to trust and to use. This case study focuses on the reseller channel. The business focus is growing revenue from top resellers: many customers still doubt a link shared in an Instagram Story or a private WhatsApp chat, so trust is where sales are lost.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

RQ1

Where in the journey does customer trust rise or drop?

RQ2

What do resellers need to share products with confidence?

RQ3

What does the EIGER team need to support more easily?

02 / Approach

Our approach: listening to all 3 user groups

Sessions were one-on-one, some online over video call and some on-site, in Indonesian. Each group first completed tasks on its own flow, then took part in an interview about the experience.

We mapped the same journey, from the first message to payout, for all three groups. This made it easier to compare how the same step is experienced by a customer, a reseller and the EIGER team.

04 / Key findings

3 key findings, one for each user group

01Customers feel unsure about a product link from a reseller they do not know yet

New customers were confused and hesitant to trust a product link sent by a reseller they had not met. They were more willing to continue when they could see who shared it, a clear preview of the product and the shop details.

Design implication: show the reseller profile and product information clearly on the link preview and the first screen.

02Resellers feel their link is not trusted: no image preview, official profile or reviews from sold products

Without an image preview, an official profile and reviews from products already sold, a shared link looks less trustworthy to buyers. Resellers want the link to show these on its own, so a chat is more likely to become an order.

Design implication: give every shared link an image preview, an official reseller profile and reviews of sold products.

03EIGER staff find it hard to help resellers because product info and order status are not in one platform

Product information and order status are spread across different places, so supporting resellers takes more effort than it should. Staff want to find both in one place and spend their time helping with real issues.

Design implication: bring product information and order status together in one platform and one view.

Each decision is based on a finding. Where I had to choose, the trade-off and the option I rejected are listed. These are recommendations to the client.

06 / Design exploration

UI concepts based on the findings

These screens are my personal UI exploration. They show how the solutions we recommended to the client could look, focusing on the two main findings: customers can see a reseller’s rating and number of reviews right before they click a link, and resellers can see and share the same trust signals. Finding 03 (the EIGER team) needs back-office and data changes, so it stays a recommendation in the client report and is not explored in UI here.

ABOUT THESE SCREENS

There was no UI design deliverable in this project. The team’s output was a prioritised list of actions for the client. These screens are my own exploration, made to project those recommended solutions into UI. They are not client work and have not been tested with users.

FINDING 01 · CUSTOMERS

Customers feel unsure about a product link from a reseller they do not know yet

Main solution: show the reseller’s rating and review count right before the link is clicked.

SCREEN 1

Rating and reviews in the link preview

PROBLEM
A product link from an unknown reseller gives no sign of whether it can be trusted.
SOLUTION
(1) The preview shows the reseller’s rating and review count, next to the official reseller badge, before the link is clicked.
WHY IT HELPS
Customers can judge trust at the moment they decide to click.
SCREEN 2

The same trust signal in Instagram Stories

PROBLEM
A link in a Story shows nothing about who is behind it.
SOLUTION
(1) The reseller’s profile and rating sit above the product. (2) The link sticker shows rating and review count.
WHY IT HELPS
Every channel the reseller uses shows the same trust signal.
SCREEN 3

The shop page confirms what the preview promised

PROBLEM
After clicking, customers still look for proof that the reseller is real.
SOLUTION
(1) Rating and review count sit in the shop header as one tappable row, next to (2) the official badge.
WHY IT HELPS
Customers keep going instead of leaving to check elsewhere.
FINDING 02 · RESELLERS

Resellers feel their link is not trusted: no image preview, official profile or reviews from sold products

Main solution: let resellers see and share the same profile, image preview and reviews that customers see.

SCREEN 4

Resellers see what buyers will see

PROBLEM
Resellers feel their link is not trusted and cannot tell how it looks to buyers.
SOLUTION
The share sheet previews what buyers will get: (1) the official profile, (2) rating and review count and (3) the product link with image.
WHY IT HELPS
Resellers can send with confidence that the link looks trustworthy.
SCREEN 5

Resellers can check their own trust profile

PROBLEM
Resellers do not know which trust signals customers see on their shop.
SOLUTION
(1) The dashboard shows rating and review count, and (2) the official badge, in one place.
WHY IT HELPS
Resellers can see and protect the signals customers rely on.

HOW THE TWO SIDES CONNECT

What resellers set up in screens 4–5 is what customers see in screens 1–3: one profile, one rating and one review count in every channel.

Scope: these concepts focus on top resellers who already have ratings and reviews. Growing new resellers is left to EIGER’s own programmes.

07 / Outcome

Results, learnings and next steps

What this produced

Findings mapped on one journey for three user groups.

Five prioritised directions, at least one for each finding.

A personal UI exploration of the recommended solutions (not a client deliverable).

What I learned

Trust matters in the whole journey, not only at checkout.

Each user group experiences the same step differently, and mapping them side by side showed which steps matter most.

Showing trade-offs early made decisions easier to discuss.

What comes next

Test the concepts with customers and resellers.

Agree simple measures first, such as confidence in sharing and ease of following an order.

Review priorities again once real usage data is available.

Impact has not been measured yet.

Customers decide whether to trust a shop before checkout, so reseller and product information must be clear from the first link.
